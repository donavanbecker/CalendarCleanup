# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

Calendar Cleanup doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal information.

## Your calendars

Calendar Cleanup reads the calendars you choose so it can find duplicate events, and it removes only the events you confirm, or that you've asked it to remove automatically when it opens. All of this happens on your device. Your calendar data is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

To let you undo a removal, the app keeps a copy of the most recently removed events in its own storage on your device. That copy is replaced by your next removal, deleted when you undo, and removed if you delete the app.

## Files you export

When you export a list of duplicates as CSV, the file is saved only where you choose.

## Network use

The App Store version, for Mac, iPhone and iPad, doesn't connect to the internet.

The Mac version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for new versions of the app. That request includes only what any web request includes, such as your IP address, and it's handled under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## No tracking

Calendar Cleanup has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Contact

Questions about privacy or the app: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/donavanbecker/CalendarCleanup/issues).
